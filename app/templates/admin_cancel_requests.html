Owner cancel requests

Approving removes the entry (maintenance or line-item corpus payments go back to unpaid; expenses and collection payments are deleted) and is audited. For an owner's common expense there are three outcomes: Approve cancel removes it, Reject expense takes it out of the expenses list, pending reimbursement and every total (the owner still sees it under My submissions as Rejected, and you can delete it from Expenses › Rejected expenses), and Keep entry leaves it counted. For other entries Reject cancel keeps the entry as it is.
